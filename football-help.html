FOOTBALL PRACTICE / 1:1 & SMALL GROUP

PRACTICE
WITH PURPOSE.

FOR PLAYERS WHO WANT MORE REPS, MORE CONFIDENCE, AND A BETTER TEAM FIT.

I play football and train with a team. I know what it’s like to want extra reps without the pressure of a full session. I can be your practice partner: one thing at a time, at your pace.

WHO
Younger players, teammates, or friends who want extra practice. All levels, teens and up.
WHAT WE’LL WORK ON
Throwing and catching, movement and positioning, decision-making under pressure, and fitness with the ball. We choose one focus per session and build it into a simple drill.
EXPERIENCE
I train and play football regularly. In 2025 I took part in training and games, working on fitness, tactics, contact, and coordination with teammates.
TIME
One hour on weekends, 1–2 sessions a month. Small groups of 2–4 are welcome.
WHAT TO BRING
Cleats, water, and a ball if you have one. I’ll bring cones and a simple plan.
HOW TO START
Use the GET IN TOUCH link on my Football page, or ask me in person. Tell me your position, one thing you want to improve, and when you’re free.
