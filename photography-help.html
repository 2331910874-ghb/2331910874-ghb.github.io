A WALK, THREE PHOTOS, AND THREE THINGS TO TRY NEXT TIME.

I take photos while travelling and between sport. I’m not a professional photographer, but I can help you slow down, notice light and colour, and make stronger choices with the camera you already have.

WHO
Friends, classmates, or travellers who want better phone or camera photos. All levels.
WHAT WE’LL DO
A walk with three things to notice: light, colour, and composition. We shoot together, then review three of your photos and agree on three changes for next time.
EXPERIENCE
My portfolio includes city, landscape, and travel images. I explain why a photo works in plain language, without jargon.
TIME
One 1–2 hour walk a month, plus about 20 minutes of feedback by message.
WHAT TO BRING
Any camera, including a phone. Comfortable shoes and one place you’ve been wanting to photograph.
HOW TO START
Use the GET IN TOUCH link on my Photography page, or ask me in person. Send three photos you like and one thing you want to improve.
